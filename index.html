About me

I am currently a statistician at Astellas Pharma. I work in the Medical Affairs statistics team, conducting clinical studies for post-regulatory approval evidence generation, publications, and reimbursement. I also co-lead a research group on Indirect Treatment Comparisons (ITCs). I work closely with stakeholders from multiple teams including medical affairs, clinical development, medical, and health economics.

Outside of my work I am a keen hiker, currently working towards my Mountain Leader qualifications. I also enjoy field hockey.

Research

My broad research area of interest is medical statistics. I am particularly interested in ITCs and their application to oncology and endocrinology. As well as co-leading an ITC research group at Astellas, I am involved with the RWE and HTA Estimands Implementation Working Group.

My primary therapeutic area of interest is pancreatic cancer, and have been involved in several clinical trials for compounds for the disease. I have also conducted some ITC work in pancreatic cancer, and have raised money for Pancreatic Cancer UK. I am also interested in endocrinology, and have been involved in several women's health studies at Astellas. I have also been involved in a recent project (to be published) on insulin-adjuvant treatments for type 1 diabetes. I have a passing interest in expedition medicine.
